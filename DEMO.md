# Pokey in 60 seconds

A script for recording the demo. **Say** is the voiceover; **Do** is what's on screen.

## Before you hit record

- Fresh browser profile (or incognito), Chill or Cheeky theme, app at http://localhost:5173.
- Enter a name on the landing page, skip the tutorial, and close the daily rundown.
- Add one task with **Photo proof** on, due a minute ago: "Buy oat milk".
- Log in to sync on the laptop, and have your phone logged in to the same email with Pokey open.
- Sound on for the mic. Each take makes about 3 real model calls.

## The script

**0:00 – 0:08 · Hook**
- Do: start on the landing page, scroll once past "Messy in, tidy out", tap Let's go.
- Say: "Reminder apps want you to be organised before they help. Pokey doesn't."

**0:08 – 0:22 · Messy dump → tidy list**
- Do: tap the mic and say: *"ugh ok, call the dentist, drink water every weekday at 8, and I need to get to Yinka's tomorrow like 5… send."*
- Do: the list fills in: the dentist with a "When should I poke you?" question, water repeating on weekdays at 8, Yinka's tomorrow at 5 PM.
- Say: "I just ramble. Pokey sorts out the times and repeats, and asks when it isn't sure."

**0:22 – 0:30 · It knows your list**
- Do: type *"actually move Yinka's to 7"* and press Enter.
- Say: "It edits what's already there. No duplicates." (Pokey: *Moved "Go to Yinka's" to tomorrow, 7:00 PM.*)

**0:30 – 0:40 · The poke**
- Do: the oat-milk poke shows on Today with Snooze and Done. Open the Pokes tab and point at Nudge → Prod → Shove.
- Say: "When something's due, Pokey pokes, with a fresh line every time, getting louder until it's done."

**0:40 – 0:48 · Photo proof**
- Do: tap Done on the oat milk → "Pics or it didn't happen?" → Snap it +15 → pick a photo → Claim. Confetti, points go up.
- Say: "For errands, show me. Plus fifteen points."

**0:48 – 0:55 · Calm mode**
- Do: type *"I'm so overwhelmed"*. The list folds to one task, with the poke-break card.
- Say: "Swamped? Say so. One thing at a time."

**0:55 – 1:00 · Sync and close**
- Do: hold up the phone. The same list is there, with oat milk ticked.
- Say: "Same list on every device. Pokey: say it messy, I'll keep it tidy."

## If something goes wrong live

- **The model is slow:** "Pokey's on it…" shows under the box and you can carry on. Fill the gap with the Pokes tab.
- **The model fails:** the local parser still adds tasks ("My brain's offline, so I winged it").
- **Wrong edit:** type "undo".
